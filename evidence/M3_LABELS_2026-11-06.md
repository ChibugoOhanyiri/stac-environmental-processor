## Reading
- **The rule carried over to unseen ground.** On the test side, which was never used to set any number, "land got louder" is 9.39% on flood day vs 0.73–0.79% on the control days (about 12×). The practice side shows the same pattern (7.52% vs 0.45–0.78%).
- **New dark water: almost none** (≤0.01%). The classic open-water sign is essentially absent in Lekki on 30 Jun at 06:29; the flood-day signal is brightening, consistent with water in built-up streets (double bounce).
- **Claim level (PRD level 2):** "On the morning of 30 June 2026, about 1.1 km² of the test area (9.4%) showed radar brightening well beyond what equally rainy and dry days show, consistent with the reported urban flooding." Not a flood extent: the labels are not checked against located flood observations yet, and brightening has other possible causes (see limits).
- **Limits:** a 50 m window can erase narrow flooded streets; brightening can also come from construction, parked vehicles or other surface changes between June and August (the compact red block left of centre in the practice side is unexplained); the dark-water line catches only a third of known water here; the east side has little permanent water (always-water label weakly tested); one wet control only; JRC ends in 2021, so newer water bodies (e.g. the keyhole-shaped lake) are not marked "always water" (they show as no change because they are dark on both dates).
