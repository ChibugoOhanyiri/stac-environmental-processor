# M3 summary: transparent water-change baseline (2026-11-07)

## In one paragraph
On the morning of 30 June 2026 (06:29, Sentinel-1D), a 5 km square of central Lekki showed radar **brightening** on about 9–10% of its land, against about 0.5–0.8% on an equally rainy day (10 Sep) and a dry day (22 Sep) compared the same way. The rules were fixed on the west half and held on the unseen east half (9.4% vs ~0.75%). The same spots lit up again in the next heavy rain (12 Jul), and the signal remained when "normal" was January instead of August. Almost no **new dark water** was seen: the textbook open-water sign is absent, and the signal is consistent with water in built-up streets (double bounce). Claim level: **measured surface change consistent with the reported flood** (PRD level 2). This is not a validated flood-extent map.

**Reading:** at all 5 places the flood day is higher than both control days, but only 2 of 5 are above their side's average. The method shows the area-wide change; it does **not** single out the named places. That is expected (a district name is not a flood boundary), but it means the point checks are weak support, not validation. Six named places (Ikota, Osapa London, Jakande, Elegushi, Lekki Phase 1, Ajah) are outside the square: the Lekki–VI corridor (S23 step 2) would include them.
**Found on the way:** OSM places the midpoint of Orchid Road about 1.5 km west of the M1 "Orchid" point (configs/case.json). Orchid Road is long, so both may be valid, but the M1 point is recorded as "area of the user's stay", not a surveyed street position.

## Main limits (carried into M4)
1. No located, timed flood observations (no flood boundary or depth): no accuracy number can be computed.
2. Brightening has other causes (construction, vehicles, wet facades); controls reduce but do not remove this.
3. 50 m smoothing can erase narrow flooded streets.
4. Dark-water rule is weak here (−17 dB catches a third of known water: wind on the lagoon).
5. One satellite pass on flood day (06:29), mid-event; rain continued until ~14:00.
6. Thresholds chosen once on one split; sensitivity to other choices still to test (M4).
