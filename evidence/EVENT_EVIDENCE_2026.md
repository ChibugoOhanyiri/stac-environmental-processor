Compiled 2026-09-27. This file answers one question: **is there dated, located evidence that flooding happened around the 30 June 2026 radar pass?** It does not prove how far the water spread (the flood *extent*). That needs a different kind of evidence, collected later (M4).

## First-hand account (context only, not scientific proof)
The project author (Chibugo) was in Lagos from **27 June to 2 July 2026** (dates corrected by the user on 2026-09-28; an earlier note said "about 2 weeks"), staying at Orchid, Lekki, and travelling often on the Lekki-Epe Expressway. They report that flooding was **already large when they arrived on 27 June** and was **still present when they left on 2 July**, and that Lekki and parts of Victoria Island were heavily flooded, with rain lasting hours to whole days and severely restricting movement. This is consistent with the news above. It is recorded as motivation and context, not as a reference measurement.

*Added 2026-09-28 (user, first-hand; stay = 27 Jun–2 Jul):* "throughout my [stay] in Lagos, the whole of Orchid was flooded, and there were floods almost everywhere in Lekki; we could hardly order food." If accurate, flooding was **persistent across both 18 Jun and 30 Jun**. That would explain why 18 Jun vs 30 Jun shows little change. *Second-hand (the user's friend, relayed 2026-09-28):* flooding had been present **from the beginning of June**. Lower weight: unverified, not first-hand; consistent with the June Coastal Highway drainage reports. Orchid Road location: 6.4317 N, 3.5566 E (OpenStreetMap/Nominatim, 2026-09-28). Chevron Drive: 6.4520 N, 3.5306 E.

## What this evidence supports — and what it does not
- **Supports:** flooding in Lekki and Victoria Island on 30 June 2026, the same morning as the radar pass, reported by several outlets and an official quoted by name.
- **Complication 1 — the "before" picture may not be dry.** Lekki was already flooding on 28 June and repeatedly through June (Coastal Highway drainage complaints). The 18 June image must be inspected, not assumed to be "normal".
- **Complication 2 — the "after" picture may not be dry.** More rain fell on 3 July. The 12 July image may still hold water.
- **Complication 3 — timing within the day.** The pass was at 06:29, while the bridge was reported submerged by 11:00. Water may still have been rising.
- **Does not support:** a flood *extent* map, flood depth, or accuracy. News names roads and districts, not boundaries.
